Understanding Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to computer systems designed to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as visual perception, decision-making, speech recognition, and language translation.

Core Pillars of Modern AI

  • Machine Learning (ML): Algorithms that learn patterns from data to make predictions.
  • Natural Language Processing (NLP): Tools that enable computers to process and understand human speech and text.
  • Computer Vision: Technologies that analyze images and video feeds.
